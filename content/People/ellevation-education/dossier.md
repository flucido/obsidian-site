## What They Do

Ellevation is the **nation's leading K-12 software solution for multilingual learners (MLs/ELs)**. The platform is a unified ELD (English Language Development) solution with four core pillars:

### 1. Identification, Placement, and Planning
- Integrates real-time student data with EL program tasks
- Compliance workflows (LPAC, reclassification, RFEP monitoring)
- Reporting tools for federal/state requirements (Title I, II, III; ESSA)

### 2. Measurement and Progress
- **Ellevation Benchmark** (launched Jan 2026): ELP assessment up to 3x/year, 20-minute assessments across speaking, listening, reading, writing
- Progress monitoring and growth tracking
- Domain-level insights for instruction adjustment

### 3. Instruction
- **Ellevation Strategies**: Digital library of 80+ research-based instructional activities
- Data-driven scaffolding and differentiation
- Planning tools for classroom teachers

### 4. Professional Learning
- Evidence-based learning modules for teacher development
- Instructional frameworks
- Progress tracking for PD programs

### Key Integrations
- **SIS platforms**: PowerSchool, Infinite Campus, Aeries, Skyward, Synergy
- **Data/assessment partners**: iReady (CA's own product), WIDA, NWEA MAP, LAS Links, TestHound, eSchoolData
- **SSO/identity**: Clever, ClassLink
- **Data protocols**: SFTP, APIs

## Business Model

- **SaaS subscription** sold to school districts (district-level deals)
- **Pricing**: Fundable via Title I, II, III federal funds; state and local sources
- **Sales model**: Direct sales to districts (inside sales + regional sales directors)
- **Customer success**: Account managers, partner success managers, data services team
- **Contract cycle**: Typically annual, district procurement cycles

### Revenue Trajectory
- Pre-acquisition: $17.5M–$50.1M raised across 5 rounds
- Series B: $10M (January 2018) — led by Chan Zuckerberg Initiative
- Investors: CZI, NewSchools Venture Fund, North Atlantic Capital, Emerson Collective, Rethink Education
- Acquired by Curriculum Associates (August 2021) — terms undisclosed
- Current: ~$37M annual revenue (Growjo estimate)
- Growth target: Double ELs served within 5 years

- Every new district needs **data integration** (SIS → Ellevation platform) — this is the critical onboarding bottleneck
