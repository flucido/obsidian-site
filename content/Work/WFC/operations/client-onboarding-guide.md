# Welcome to WellFull Collective — What to Expect

> This document walks you through our process from signing to launch.
> It's designed to give you clarity on timeline, milestones, and what we need from you — so there are no surprises.

---

## How We Work

We're a two-person team with distinct roles:

| Person | Role | What They Handle |
|--------|------|-----------------|
| **Lauren Ogren, PsyD, MFT** | Clinical + Content Lead | Understands your practice, your clients, and your voice. Leads the content strategy — what your site says and how it speaks to the people you want to reach. |
| **Frank Lucido** | Design + Technical Lead | Translates your brand into design. Builds the site, handles SEO/GEO, and makes sure people can actually find you. |

You'll work with Lauren first on content strategy, then Frank on design and build. But Lauren stays involved throughout — she's not just a first-meeting person. She'll join the build review and launch handoff so you always have access to both of us. Her content strategy feeds directly into Frank's design work, and she's there to answer clinical and practice questions at any stage.

---

## The Process (4–6 Weeks Total)

### Phase 1: Content Strategy (Week 1)
**One 30-minute meeting with Lauren**

**Meeting 1 — Discovery:** This is where we define what your site needs to say. Lauren will ask about:
- Your ideal client — who are you trying to reach?
- Your therapeutic approach — what makes your practice different?
- Your current referral sources — what's working and what's missing?
- The tone and feeling you want the site to convey

After this meeting, you'll have time to sit with the discussion, review any notes or feedback, and let things percolate. Lauren will follow up with you during the design phase (see Phase 2) for a second conversation to refine the direction before we build.

**You'll leave this phase with:** a clear initial brand voice and content direction — the starting point for your site's story.

**What we need from you after your Discovery session with Lauren (within 2 weeks):**

We don't expect you to have all of this ready before we talk. Your conversation with Lauren will help you clarify what you want. After your session, gather the following:

- **Your content vision** — In your own words, what do you want someone to feel when they visit your site? What's the one thing you want a potential client to understand about you? Lauren will help you articulate this during your session, but it helps if you start thinking about it now.

- **Photos** — Professional headshots are ideal, but don't let that stop you. A clear, well-lit photo of you goes a long way. If you have photos from your office, your space, or your work environment, those help too. If you don't have professional photos yet, we can recommend photographers who work with therapists — or we can work with what you have and plan a session later.

- **Anything you want on the site** — This is where you tell us what matters to you. Maybe there's a particular image, a quote, a color, a feeling from another website you love. Maybe you have a specific page in mind — a resources section, a blog, a page for a particular specialty. Maybe you want a video. Maybe you have nothing specific yet and that's fine too. The point is: if you have a vision for any piece of this, tell us. We'll work with it. If you don't, Lauren will help you find it.

- **Anything you want to keep** — If you have existing content (a bio, service descriptions, a blog post, a Psychology Today profile you've spent time on), share it. We may not use it as-is, but it tells us how you already talk about your work — and that's valuable.

Don't overthink this. You don't need to show up with a mood board or a Pinterest page (though if you have one, great). Just come to your Lauren session ready to talk honestly about your practice and what you want your site to do for you. We'll take it from there.

---

### Phase 2: Design (Weeks 1–3)
**Led by Frank — with Lauren's follow-up woven in**

During the design phase, Lauren will schedule a second 30-minute call with you — **Meeting 2, Revision & Refinement.** This happens while Frank is working on the visual direction, so your feedback and refinements feed directly into the design. Bring your thoughts, new ideas, questions that came up after Discovery, and anything you've gathered (photos, content, references). We'll schedule this for whatever works best for you during weeks 1–3.

Meanwhile, Frank takes Lauren's content strategy and builds the visual direction. The design review is a working session, not a quick check-in — we'll spend a full hour together so there's real time to explore, sketch, and iterate.

- Design mockups — you'll see what your site will look like before we build anything
- Design system tokens — your colors, typography, and spacing, documented so everything stays consistent
- Up to 2 review cycles of structural revisions — additional rounds are billed at our hourly rate to keep the project on track.

**Milestone:** You approve the design direction. We don't build until you're happy with how it looks.

---

### Phase 3: Build (Weeks 3–5)
**Frank builds. You review.**

Frank leads development, content loading, and configuration. Lauren is present for the build review — she's there for any clinical or practice questions that come up as you see the site take shape.

- All pages built and populated with your content (up to 10 core pages and 3 blog posts included; additional content migration billed separately).
- Mobile-responsive (looks great on phones and tablets).
- Accessible (WCAG AA — readable by screen readers, navigable by keyboard).
- **SEO & GEO Foundation:** We go beyond standard technical metadata. We architect your content for generative engines (AI search) by building topical authority and structuring information to answer natural language queries directly, ensuring your practice is surfaced as a trusted, expert resource in AI-driven summaries.
- Google Business Profile setup or alignment.
- Consult booking form integrated (**Note:** You are responsible for providing a HIPAA-compliant form service, such as a compliant tier of Google Workspace or Hushmail, and executing necessary BAAs).
- Psychology Today profile alignment (so both surfaces work together).

You'll get a preview link to review the live site before it goes public.

---

### Phase 4: Review + Launch (Week 5–6)
**Final polish and go-live — Frank + Lauren**

Frank leads the final review and launch, with Lauren present for any last clinical or content questions.

- Final review and adjustments
- Domain connection (or new domain setup)
- Launch — your site is live
- 30 days of post-launch support begins (bug fixes, minor adjustments, questions)

---

## Project Timelines & Client Feedback

Our 4–6 week timeline relies on timely collaboration. If your feedback, content gathering, or review cycles are delayed by more than 7 days, the project timeline will be automatically paused. Restarting the project after a pause may be subject to a scheduling delay to accommodate our ongoing queue.

## How Often We Meet

| Meeting                         | When        | Duration  | With                   |
| ------------------------------- | ----------- | --------- | ---------------------- |
| Content Strategy — Discovery    | Week 1      | 30 min    | Lauren                 |
| Content Strategy — Revision     | Weeks 1–3   | 30 min    | Lauren                 |
| Design Review (working session) | Weeks 2–3   | 60 min    | Frank                  |
| Build Review                    | Week 4–5    | 30-60 min | Frank                  |
| Launch Handoff                  | Week 6      | 30 min    | Frank (Lauren present) |

We keep meetings short and focused. Most communication happens async — you'll get updates as we hit milestones, and you can reach us anytime with questions.

**Outside the defined process:** If you want additional time with Lauren at any stage — whether it's a deeper clinical consultation, content brainstorming, or practice strategy — that is available at her hourly rate (detailed in your Statement of Work), separate from the website contract. Just ask.

## What Signing Looks Like

1. **You approve the proposal** — scope, timeline, and investment are all spelled out. No surprises.
2. **50% deposit** — due on signing. Work begins once the deposit clears.
3. **Content strategy session scheduled** — within 1 week of deposit.
4. **We're off.** Lauren meets with you, then Frank takes over.

The remaining 50% is due at launch, when your site goes live.

---

## After Launch — What Support Looks Like

- **30 days of included support:** bug fixes, minor adjustments, questions — anything that comes up right after launch.
- **Monthly support (optional):** if you want us to keep managing updates, content changes, and maintenance, we offer an ongoing retainer at $100–250/month. This is optional — you're never locked in.
- **You own the site.** On full payment, the website and all content are yours. We provide the design tokens so your brand stays consistent even if someone else works on it later.
- **Please note** that we are not a website hosting provider and do not host websites. We are happy to assist you with setting up your hosting, which can be incorporated into our project contract or your monthly retainer agreement.
