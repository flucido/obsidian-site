# Therapist Website Engagement Guide

> How a WellFull Collective project works — from first conversation to launch day.
> For use in discovery calls and follow-up emails with prospective clients.

---

## How We Work

We design websites for therapists that feel like your practice — not a template, not a generic wellness site, and not something that makes you cringe when you send someone the link.

The process is simple. We handle the design, the build, and the technical setup. You bring your content vision and your clinical perspective. Lauren (PsyD, MFT) is involved throughout — she understands the clinical side because she lives it.

---

## The Three Options

### Option 1: Landing Page — $1,500
A single-page site that introduces who you are, what you do, and how to reach you. Fast turnaround. Good for therapists who need something professional right now and aren't ready for a full site.

**What you get:**
- One scrollable page with 4-6 sections
- Mobile-responsive design
- Contact form or booking link
- Basic SEO setup
- Launch within 2 weeks of content delivery

### Option 2: CMS Site — $3,500 + $100-250/mo
A full practice website with a content management system. You can update your own content (blog posts, availability, new services) without calling a developer. This is what most therapists choose.

**What you get:**
- Up to 10 core pages and 3 blog posts
- Content management system (you can edit your own site)
- SEO + GEO (generative engine optimization) setup
- Google Business Profile integration
- 2 review cycles included
- Monthly hosting, maintenance, and support ($100-250/mo)
- Launch within 4-6 weeks of content delivery

### Option 3: Custom Web Application — Custom Quote
For practices with specific needs: online intake forms, client portals, scheduling integration, multi-practitioner directories. We scope this based on what you actually need.

## What We Need From You

We don't need you to write your website. We need you to tell us about your practice. Here's what helps:

1. **Your content vision** — What do you want people to feel when they land on your site? What's the tone? What should they know about you?
2. **Photos** — Professional headshots if you have them, or photos of your space. If you don't have any, we can work with stock imagery temporarily.
3. **Anything you want on the site** — Testimonials (with permission), articles you've written, specializations you want to highlight, modalities you use.
4. **Anything you want to keep off** — Some therapists don't want certain topics mentioned, or prefer not to list specific diagnoses. Tell us your boundaries.

Lauren helps with content strategy. She'll talk with you about what to include and how to frame it. You don't need to have this figured out before we start.

## The Process

### Phase 1: Discovery (Week 1)
- Lauren leads a 30-minute content call with you
- We ask about your practice, your clients, your goals
- You share whatever materials you have (photos, existing site, content ideas)

### Phase 2: Design & Build (Weeks 2-4)
- We design and build your site
- You review at key milestones (not a surprise reveal at the end)
- Up to 2 review cycles of structural revisions are included
- Additional review rounds billed at Lauren's hourly rate

### Phase 3: Review & Launch (Week 5-6)
- Final review with you and Lauren
- SEO/GEO setup
- Google Business Profile connection
- Launch day

### After Launch
- Monthly maintenance and support (CMS Site tier)
- Content updates you can do yourself through the CMS
- Ongoing SEO monitoring

## The Fine Print

- **Scope cap**: Up to 10 core pages and 3 blog posts included in the CMS Site tier. Additional content migration billed separately.
- **Review cycles**: 2 structural revision rounds included. Additional rounds at hourly rate.
- **Pause policy**: If we don't hear from you for 7+ business days during the build, we pause the project. When you're ready to resume, we pick up where we left off. No penalty — life happens.
- **HIPAA note**: If your site includes a consult booking form, you are responsible for ensuring your booking service is HIPAA-compliant and that you have the appropriate Business Associate Agreements in place. WellFull Collective is not a covered entity.
- **Hosting**: WellFull Collective is not a hosting provider. We set up and manage your site on our infrastructure, but we don't guarantee 100% uptime SLAs. We do guarantee that if something breaks, we fix it.
- **Pricing**: All prices are in the engagement agreement. Retainer pricing for ongoing work is defined in the Statement of Work, not in marketing materials.
