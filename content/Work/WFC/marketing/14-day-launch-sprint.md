# WFC 14-Day Launch Sprint — Content + Social + SEO

> Synthesizes [[social-media-campaign]], [[content-strategy-social-seo]], and
> [[launch-checklist]] into a single execution timeline. The 14-day window is
> a deliberate choice — explained below.

---

## Why 14 Days (Not 7, Not 30)

| Window | Why Not |
|--------|---------|
| **7 days** | Too compressed. Can't batch 2 weeks of content + set up accounts + build templates + test the consult form funnel in one week. Rushed launch = weak first impression that's hard to recover from. |
| **30 days** | Unnecessary delay. Every day the campaign isn't running = zero inbound = zero revenue. The existing strategy, content drafts, and checklist are already done — we're not inventing, we're executing. |
| **14 days** | Right-sized. Days 1–7 for all setup and batching (accounts, templates, Week 1–2 content). Days 8–14 for launch + live iteration + first data loop. Two weeks of content banked before the first post goes live — no scrambling. |

The core logic: **batch ahead, launch fast, iterate on real data.** The 14-day window front-loads production so the launch week is calm and the second week is data-driven.

## Phase 1: Setup Sprint (Days 1–7)

Goal: everything that needs to exist before a single post goes live.

### Day 1–2: Accounts & Infrastructure
- Create IG Business account + TikTok Business account
- Set up link-in-bio tool (brand colors, consult form link, UTM params)
- Create LinkedIn Company Page + announcement post drafted
- Set up UTM tracking: `utm_source=instagram&utm_medium=social&utm_campaign=launch-wkX`
- Add social links to wellfullcollective.com footer + header
- Verify consult form → Resend email → inbox flow end-to-end

### Day 3–4: Content Templates & Brand Kit
- Build Canva/Figma template file: IG carousel (1080x1080, 5-slide max), IG story (1080x1920), TikTok (1080x1920)
- Brand colors enforced: terracotta #ead5c8, sage #dae8df, espresso #5a3e2e. Warm blush #ffc1d2 for accent only.
- Fonts: Cormorant Garamond (headlines), Atkinson Hyperlegible (body)
- Pre-shoot Week 1 TikTok batch (3 videos: Mon/Wed/Fri)
- Pre-design Week 1 IG carousels (2 carousels + 1 single image per week)
- Write all Week 1 captions, hashtags, and queue in scheduling tool

### Day 5–6: Content Batching — Week 1 + Week 2
- Week 1 content (Campaign Theme: "The Problem"): finalized, reviewed, scheduled
  - Mon IG: "83% of therapist websites use DIY templates" carousel
  - Wed TikTok: "3 signs your website is overwhelming clients" talking head
  - Fri IG Story: "Did you build your own website?" poll sequence
- Week 2 content (Campaign Theme: "The Science"): drafted, reviewed, scheduled
  - Mon IG: "Processing fluency: why cognitive ease precedes engagement" single image
  - Wed TikTok: "The 4-second rule" text overlay
  - Fri IG Story: "Have you ever felt something was off?" Q&A sticker
- Parallel: 3 website tips posts drafted from [[content-strategy-social-seo]] Track 1
  - Stock photo carousel ("Your website uses the same stock photo as 200 other sites")
  - Photography tips TikTok ("You don't need a pro — you need these 5 phone camera tips")
  - "3 things to remove from your homepage today" IG carousel

### Day 7: Pre-Launch Verification
- Human review: ALL posts, captions, and visuals approved
- Consult form tested end-to-end with a test submission
- UTM links verified (click through to consult form, params preserved)
- Analytics baseline captured (current: 0 followers, 0 visits — this is the zero point)
- LinkedIn announcement post drafted + approved
- Go/no-go decision from human

## Phase 2: Launch + Iterate (Days 8–14)

Goal: go live, watch the data, adjust.

### Day 8: LAUNCH
- LinkedIn announcement post published (Frank's profile → tags @WellFullCollective)
- IG Week 1 content begins (first carousel goes live)
- TikTok Week 1 content begins (first video goes live)
- Link-in-bio updated with "New: our launch post" CTA

### Day 9–11: Week 1 Live — Watch & Respond
- Monitor: follower count, post engagement (likes/comments/shares), link-in-bio clicks, consult form submissions
- Respond to every comment within 2 hours (warmth = brand signal)
- Track which post format performs best: carousel vs. single image vs. talking head vs. poll
- Daily 5-minute check-in: any consult requests? Any warm-signal therapist engagement?

### Day 12–14: Week 2 Live — First Data Loop
- Week 2 content goes live (campaign theme shifts to "The Science")
- Website tips content enters rotation (parallel track begins)
- Week 1 data analyzed: what worked, what didn't
  - If carousels outperform: double down for Week 3
  - If TikTok outperforms: shift IG budget of effort toward TT
  - If link-in-bio clicks are low: test different CTA language
- Adjust Week 3 content plan based on real data (not assumptions)

### End of Day 14: First Retrospective
- Metrics snapshot: followers, weekly visits, consult requests, consult-to-proposal ratio
- Decision: continue as planned, or pivot based on data
- If zero consults: diagnose — is it reach (not enough followers) or conversion (CTA/consult form friction)?
- If 1+ consults: celebrate, analyze what drove it, double down

|------|---------------|-------------------|-------------------|
| 1 | The Problem | Stock photo + homepage fixes | — |
| 2 | The Science | Photography tips + font/typography | — |

After Week 4: transition to a sustainable 3x/week cadence with monthly content island drops.

---

## Why This Order (Not Any Other)

1. **Social before SEO.** AI agents won't cite a site with zero backlinks and zero brand presence. Social content builds the surface area that SEO tools index. Content islands and llms.txt in Weeks 3-4 have something to reference.
2. **Campaign narrative before website tips.** The 4-week Problem→Science→Solution→Studio arc establishes who WFC is and why you should care. Website tips (stock photos, photography, quick wins) are the "useful now" content that earns follows, but they work better when the brand narrative is already in the feed.
3. **Batch before launch.** The single biggest risk is launching and immediately falling behind on content production. Days 1-7 eliminate that risk by banking 2 weeks of posts before the first one goes live.
4. **Data loop at Day 14, not Day 7.** One week isn't enough signal. Two weeks gives you: a full campaign theme transition (Problem→Science), enough posts for engagement patterns to emerge, and at least one weekend cycle (when therapists browse social media).
