# WFC Social Media Campaign — IG + TikTok Inbound Strategy

> **This is the primary lead generation engine for WFC.** Replaces cold email outreach
> (retired 2026-06-01). All traffic flows: social content → link-in-bio →
> wellfullcollective.com/consult → consult form → qualified lead.

### **Campaign Strategy Pivot**

* **Shift to Inbound Empathy:** Transition from cold outreach to a warmth-first social media model that attracts therapists by educating and validating their concerns.  
* **Targeting the Unspoken Friction:** Center the messaging around the core thesis that design must regulate before it converts, directly speaking to practitioners who sense their DIY websites feel wrong but cannot articulate why.  
* **Privacy as a Clinical Standard:** Frame technical infrastructure not as a feature, but as an extension of clinical safety. We emphasize that secure, compliant systems protect vulnerable clients from invasive tracking.
