# WFC Intro Content — Video + Static Post Briefs

> Four assets introducing WellFull Collective. All featuring Frank + Lauren on camera
> (or as the design subject for statics). Everything on brand: terracotta, sage, espresso,
> Cormorant Garamond + Atkinson Hyperlegible. Tone: warm, unhurried, clinically informed.

---

## Asset 1: TikTok Intro Video (60–90 sec)

**Format:** Talking head — Frank + Lauren sit side by side. Warm, real environment (not a studio).
**Vibe:** Two people who actually work together, not a polished ad. Think "sitting down with you for 60 seconds."

### Visual Setup
- **Background:** Neutral warm wall or bookshelf. One plant. No clutter.
- **Lighting:** Soft, warm — natural window light or a single warm lamp. No ring light harshness.
- **Wardrobe:** Relaxed professional. Lauren in earth tones, Frank in muted slate or olive.
- **Framing:** Medium shot, both visible from the chest up. Slight offset (not dead center) — asymmetric, calm.

### Script

**[0:00–0:08] — Lauren opens**
> "I'm a licensed therapist. And for years, I watched my colleagues spend thousands on websites that felt… wrong. Too busy. Too loud. Too salesy."

**[0:08–0:18] — Frank picks up**
> "And I spent 20 years building systems for organizations where trust wasn't optional — schools, nonprofits, public institutions. When Lauren told me what therapists were dealing with online, I couldn't unsee it."

**[0:18–0:35] — Together, alternating**
> Lauren: "The people visiting your website aren't casually shopping. They're anxious. They're scanning for safety."
> Frank: "Most templates are built to sell products to people with energy to burn. That's not who shows up on a therapist's site."
> Lauren: "So we built something different."

**[0:35–0:55] — The pitch, spoken like a shared conviction**
> Lauren: "WellFull Collective is a web design studio where a clinician and an engineer build every site together."
> Frank: "She makes sure the words and the feel are right. I make sure the thing actually works — fast, private, secure."
> Lauren: "Your website should regulate someone before it asks them to book."

**[0:55–1:05] — CTA, casual**
> Frank: "If that sounds like what you've been looking for —"
> Lauren: "— link in bio. We do a free consult. No pressure."
> *(both relax, maybe a small smile)*

### On-Screen Text (TikTok captions)
- Bold key phrases in espresso `#5a3e2e` on terracotta `#ead5c8` highlight bars
- Font: Atkinson Hyperlegible, large, mobile-readable
- Keywords: "licensed therapist," "20 years," "anxious," "regulate," "free consult"

### Hashtags
`#TherapistWebsites #PrivatePractice #WebDesign #TherapyBusiness #WellFullCollective`

## Production Notes

### Filming Both Videos (Same Session)
- Film TikTok and Reel back-to-back in one session
- TikTok: more casual, longer, conversational — film first (warms you up)
- Reel: tighter, more polished, shorter — film second
- Same location, same lighting, same wardrobe
- Budget 90 minutes total (30 min setup, 30 min TikTok, 30 min Reel)

### For Static Posts
- Use Canva or Figma with the exact hex values above
- Cormorant Garamond available free from Google Fonts
- Atkinson Hyperlegible available free from the Braille Institute
- Rounded corners on all text blocks (use `1.5rem` radius to match site)
- No stock photos — use real headshots or the initials-on-rose-gold placeholder pattern from the site

### Brand Consistency Checklist
- [ ] All text uses Cormorant Garamond (headlines) or Atkinson Hyperlegible (body)
- [ ] No colors outside the palette (terracotta, sage, espresso, slate, rose gold, oat)
- [ ] No urgency language ("limited time," "don't miss out," "act now")
- [ ] No dark patterns or manipulation
- [ ] Tone: warm, unhurried, clinically informed, never salesy
- [ ] CTA always framed as invitation, not pressure
