## What We're Working On

### 1. Lucido Technology Consulting (LTC) — Educational Data Infrastructure

**What we do:** Help California K-12 school districts take control of their data. Most districts run on a patchwork of proprietary systems (student information systems, assessment platforms, compliance tools) where they don't actually own or control their own data pipeline. We provide data infrastructure consulting — audits, ETL pipeline design, SIS migration support, CALPADS compliance, and open-source data stack implementation.

**Why it matters:** Districts spend $50K–$100K+ on data management but often can't answer basic questions about their own data flow. FERPA and California's AB 1584 add compliance requirements most districts aren't equipped to handle. Meanwhile, they're locked into vendor ecosystems with no exit strategy.

### 2. WellFull Collective (WFC) — Therapist Website Design

**What we do:** Design and build websites for private-practice therapists (PsyD, LMFT, LCSW) that actually work for their clients. Most therapist websites are DIY template jobs — 83% in Marin County alone — that overwhelm anxious clients before they even book a consult. Our approach is grounded in neuroaesthetic research: design that regulates the nervous system before it tries to convert.

**Product:** A single-page marketing site is live at wellfullcollective.com (Next.js, deployed on Vercel). Three service tiers:
- Landing Page ($1,500) — single-page site, fast turnaround
- CMS Site ($3,500 + $100–250/mo) — full practice website with content management
- Web Application (custom quote) — custom web apps

**Current state:** The product is built. The consult form is live. The marketing strategy was recently pivoted from cold outreach to inbound-only (social media → website → consult form). A 4-week Instagram/TikTok content calendar is drafted but not yet launched. The pre-launch checklist is ready — we need someone to help launch it.

**Market intelligence:** We scraped the Marin County CAMFT directory (571 therapists, 364 with websites) and built 20 scored dossiers with platform audits. The competitive landscape is wide open — no dominant design agency in the therapist vertical. The opportunity is real; we just haven't started sending signal yet.

### 3. Shared Infrastructure — The Operating System

Both orgs run on a shared operational layer that I've built over the past several weeks:

- **Knowledge base:** An Obsidian vault that serves as the company brain — pipelines, dashboards, templates, research, daily notes, decision logs. Everything links together.
- **Operational cadence:** Daily startup → standup → revenue block → delivery → strategic block → evening reflection. Fully templated.
- **Finance layer:** Pricing catalog, proposal templates, contract terms (including FERPA/AB 1584 for LTC), invoice templates, revenue dashboard.
- **Custom tooling:** A Company Dashboard Obsidian plugin that surfaces pipeline health, metrics, and daily actions.
- **Content pipeline:** Blog → LinkedIn draft auto-generation built for the frank-lucido-site (Payload CMS with custom afterChange hooks).
