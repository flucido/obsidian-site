### **Mosyle Product Overview: The "Apple Unified Platform"**

Mosyle is a specialized Mobile Device Management (MDM) and security solution designed specifically for Apple ecosystems (macOS, iOS, tvOS). They have a dedicated focus on K-12 education, which is highly relevant for North Bridge Academy.

#### **Key Features for Schools:**

* **Unified Platform:** Integrates device management, endpoint security, internet filtering, and identity management into a single dashboard.  
* **Zero-Touch Deployment:** Devices can be shipped directly to staff or students and automatically configured upon first boot.  
* **Classroom Management:** Tools for teachers to manage student iPads/Macs (e.g., locking to a specific app, screen sharing).  
* **Security & Privacy:** Includes Mac-specific antivirus, automated hardening/compliance templates, and encrypted DNS-based web filtering.  
* **Cost-Effectiveness:** Historically significantly cheaper than competitors like Jamf (often cited as low as $0.46/device/month for schools).
