## Stage Definitions

- **New**: Initial contact received, not yet qualified
- **Qualified**: District decision-maker engaged, needs assessed
- **Proposal**: Proposal submitted, awaiting response
- **Negotiation**: Terms discussion active
- **Won**: Contract signed
- **Lost**: Opportunity closed without win
