# LinkedIn Post 2 — "FERPA-Certified" Is a Marketing Fiction

**Source:** "Navigating the California Student Data Privacy Stack" — doc #3
**Funnel:** Consideration
**Cluster:** K-12 Data Sovereignty

---

Every edtech vendor says they're "FERPA-certified."

Here's the problem: FERPA certification doesn't exist.

It's a marketing term. Not a legal designation. Not a compliance standard. A phrase invented to make procurement departments stop asking questions.

---

And in California, FERPA is the floor — not the ceiling.

California has **7 layers** of student data privacy law. Most vendors address exactly one:

FERPA — federal baseline
PPRA — pupil rights
COPPA — children under 13
SOPIPA — bans student profiling & commercial data use
AB 1584 — mandates district possession of records
CCPA — consumer privacy applied to education
AB 2273 — "privacy by default" for minors

When a Marin County auditor asks to see how your district handles student data, "our vendor says they're FERPA-certified" doesn't satisfy layers 4 through 7.

They want code-level access logs. Deterministic query tracking. Verifiable proof.

Not a green checkmark on a vendor dashboard.

---

Compliance isn't a procurement checkbox. It's an operational discipline — one your district either owns or outsources to a promise.

Which one holds up when the auditor walks in?
