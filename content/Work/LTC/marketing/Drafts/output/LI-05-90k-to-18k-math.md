# LinkedIn Post 5 — The $90K → $18K Math

**Source:** "Strategic Alignment" — doc #2
**Funnel:** Consideration → Decision
**Cluster:** K-12 Data Sovereignty

---

Here's the math that should make every school board member lean forward in their chair.

---

A district of 1,700 students. Traditional cloud analytics stack — Azure, managed services, licensing, storage, compute. **$90,000 per year.**

Same analytical capability on an open-source stack — DuckDB, dbt, Dagster, Metabase, running on a single local server. **Under $18,000 per year.**

That's an 84% cost reduction.

---

But the real savings aren't the line item.

The real savings are what *stops happening*:

No more weeks-long vendor ticket queues for a simple CALPADS extract.

No more "Excel Night" — analysts manually reconciling CSVs before every board meeting because the "convenient" system can't produce the report.

No more forced upgrades that break internal workflows on the vendor's timeline, not yours.

No more paying for cloud compute cycles to process data that could live on a server in your own building.

---

You're not just cutting a vendor invoice.

You're eliminating operational friction that compounds every quarter, every board cycle, every reporting deadline.

The question isn't whether your district can afford to evaluate open-source infrastructure.

It's whether you can afford to keep paying the hyperscaler tax on data you already own.
