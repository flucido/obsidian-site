# LinkedIn Post 1 — The Support Ticket Trap

**Source:** "Beyond the Proprietary Black Box" — doc #1
**Funnel:** Awareness
**Cluster:** K-12 Data Sovereignty

---

Your district pays $50K/year for a vendor with a "throat to choke."

Here's what actually happens when something breaks.

---

It's Wednesday afternoon. Your board meeting is Thursday morning. You need an enrollment trend dashboard — five years of data, broken down by school site.

Your SIS module crashes. You submit a tier-2 support ticket.

"Expected response time: 24 to 48 hours."

Thursday morning arrives. No dashboard. Your data analyst spent Wednesday night manually cross-referencing CSV exports in Excel — exactly the work the $50K/year system was supposed to eliminate.

The vendor didn't remove the work. They buried it behind a bureaucracy.

This is the **Support Ticket Trap**. Most districts are in it right now and don't know it — because the analyst pulling the all-nighter doesn't make it into the board report.

---

The alternative isn't chaos. It's a deterministic code pipeline.

Three lines in DuckDB. An answer in two seconds. Running on hardware you already own.

Convenience sold as a product.
Control built as a practice.

One leaves your analysts reconciling spreadsheets at midnight.
The other lets them go home.
