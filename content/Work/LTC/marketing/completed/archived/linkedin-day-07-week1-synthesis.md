# Day 7 LinkedIn Post — Week 1 Synthesis

The cheapest way to find out if you have procurement debt: read the termination clause of your most expensive EdTech contract.

This week I walked through what happens when a district's data infrastructure is owned by someone else. The Support Ticket Trap — $50K a year for an SLA that reschedules analyst labor into Wednesday nights. Data debt that's actually procurement debt with a spreadsheet addiction. The moment a DPA voids itself because the vendor got acquired and nobody read page 14.

Four patterns emerged across every district I've worked with:

1. The vendor SLA doesn't remove work — it reschedules it into the hours when error rates are highest.
2. Data "ownership" is an architectural claim, not a legal one. If the format is proprietary, you're a tenant.
3. The convenience tax compounds. Year one looks great. Year five is an analyst in Excel reconstructing exports the platform was sold to produce.
4. Auditors are getting smarter. A vendor's compliance dashboard is not evidence. Code-level logs are.
