# Day 6 LinkedIn Post — The Reliability Myth

The "safest" choice in EdTech procurement is the one most likely to disappear in an acquisition.

Three deprecations in the last 36 months:

→ A major SIS provider discontinued its legacy reporting module. Districts that built their entire CALPADS workflow on it had 90 days to migrate. The replacement product cost 40% more and didn't support half the custom reports districts relied on.

→ An analytics platform acquired by private equity sunset its on-premise offering within 18 months. Districts were migrated to a cloud-only tier with different data residency. Their SOPIPA posture changed overnight.

→ A popular assessment tool stopped supporting CSV exports — API-only. Districts without in-house developers lost access to their own longitudinal data. The alternative was a $12K professional services engagement.

In every case, the vendor was "safe" at procurement. Market leader. Reference customers. Industry standard.

Vendor stability is a story told by sales decks. Code ownership is a fact written in your infrastructure. One survives acquisitions. The other doesn't.
