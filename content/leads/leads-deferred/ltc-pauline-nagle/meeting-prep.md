## Discussion Flow (Suggested)

### Opening (5 min)
> "Thanks for reaching out — what drew you to connect? What's your world look like right now with Anothos and Loretta?"

Let her lead. She initiated the connection — understand why.

### Her Story — The New Morning School Experience (10 min)
> "I saw your podcast with MoonshotOS about the school closure. That's a chapter most people never talk about publicly. What did that experience clarify for you about what schools actually need?"

She's spoken publicly about it — it's fair game. This shows you've done your homework and respect her experience.

### Current Work — Where She Operates (10 min)
> "What kinds of schools are reaching out to you? What's the most common operational challenge you're walking into?"

This surfaces her client profile, her methodology, and potential overlap with LTC.

### Frank's Space — Where LTC Operates (5 min)
> "I work on the public district side — California K-12. SIS migrations, student data pipelines, CALPADS compliance. Different world from independent schools in some ways, but the data challenges rhyme. A 30,000-student district and a 300-student independent school both have to answer: where's the data, who can see it, and is it right?"

Bridge to your work naturally. Don't make it a pitch — make it a comparison.

### Intersection (10 min)
> "I'm curious — when you're helping a school get operations and mission aligned, does data infrastructure ever come up? Or do you find schools just aren't there yet?"

Probe for overlap. If she says yes, explore. If no, that's fine.

> "Loretta is interesting to me. The tech management layer for schools — that's the part most districts don't have either. How do schools actually use it?"

Shows interest in her work. Opens door for Loretta conversation.

### What's Next (5 min)
> "I'd love to stay connected. Is there a world where what you do and what I do complement each other for a school? What does your ideal referral look like?"

Close with openness. Don't force a specific ask — let the relationship develop.

## Company Deep Dive — Three Organizations (Read Before Meeting)

Full deep dive on Anothos Collaborative + Loretta Technologies + MoonshotOS: [[company-deep-dive]]

Key takeaways for the conversation:

1. **There's a stack forming.** MoonshotOS (Peter Baron) = strategy/operations methodology for independent schools. Anothos (Pauline) = operational clarity/systems consulting + tutoring/advocacy. Loretta (Ted Serbinski) = IT management platform (devices/apps/networks). Three layers, same market (independent schools), complementary not competitive.

2. **Anothos is in transition.** The website (anothos.org) is built on the old New Morning School Wix site — the /give page is still New Morning School fundraising content (donations, scholarship fund, Boowl-a-Thon). The consulting practice is layered on top of legacy school infrastructure. She's building something new from the remains of something that ended.

3. **Anothos has three service lines, not just consulting.** (1) Educator Consulting — leadership coaching, professional learning, strategic collaboration for schools/orgs. (2) Parent Support — guidance and advocacy for families navigating school systems. (3) Summer Academy — small-group academic enrichment. Plus tutoring and student advocacy. This is broader than the LinkedIn headline suggests — she's not just a B2B consultant, she's running a hybrid education services org.

4. **Loretta is NOT a data company.** They do device/app/network management + co-managed IT support + policy guidance. They do not do SIS, CALPADS, data pipelines, or analytics. That gap is where LTC lives.

5. **MoonshotOS is a consulting firm, not tech.** The "School Operating System" is a management framework (annual priorities, 90-day sprints, weekly cadence, scorecards) — not software. Peter Baron is the brand; 20+ years in independent schools, 200+ head conversations, podcast with 1,200+ newsletter subscribers.

6. **Pauline is the bridge.** She advises Loretta (school leader perspective on the platform) and has been on MoonshotOS's podcast. Her Anothos consulting sits between strategy (MoonshotOS) and execution (Loretta).

7. **The fractional services trend is a signal.** MoonshotOS's recent episode covers fractional services driving 40% enrollment growth at one school. Schools are increasingly buying outsourced expertise. This validates LTC's consulting model.

8. **LTC is the data layer none of them have.** Neither MoonshotOS nor Loretta does data infrastructure. When a school needs SIS work, data migrations, reporting, or analytics — Frank is the referral destination.
