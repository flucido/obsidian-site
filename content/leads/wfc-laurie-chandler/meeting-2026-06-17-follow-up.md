## 2. What a CMS Is (In Plain English)

You asked what CMS means. Here's the simplest way to think about it:

**CMS = the admin area where you edit your own website.**

Right now, your WordPress site has a dashboard where you go to change things — edit text, add pages, upload photos. A CMS is the same idea, just simpler and built specifically for what you need.

When we build your site, we'd create a custom admin area with just the pieces you actually use:
- A place to edit your homepage text
- A place to update your services or specialties
- A place to write and publish blog posts (if you want a blog)
- A place to swap out photos
- A place to update your contact info

No WordPress plugins, no theme editors, no confusing menus. Just the fields you need. You log in, make your change, hit save, and it's live.

You wouldn't need to know any code. If you can write an email, you can update your website.

**What we'd use:** One of the options we prefer is a tool called Payload — it's open-source, which means there are no licensing fees to you. We'd customize the admin panel to match exactly what your practice needs. You'd only see what's relevant to your site, not a generic dashboard with 50 options you'll never touch.
