## Background

### Career

Jim Siegl is one of the most recognized voices in **K-12 student data privacy** in the United States.

- **Future of Privacy Forum (FPF)** — Senior Fellow, Youth & Education Privacy team. FPF is the leading nonprofit focused on data privacy in the digital age, with a dedicated education privacy practice.
- **Fairfax County Public Schools (FCPS)** — Technology Architect for nearly two decades. FCPS is the 10th largest school district in the US (~180,000 students). His focus: privacy, security, identity management, interoperability, and learning management systems.
- **CoSN (Consortium for School Networking)** — Co-author of the **CoSN Privacy Toolkit** and the **Trusted Learning Environment (TLE) seal program**. These are the gold-standard frameworks for K-12 data privacy practices. Received CoSN's 2013 award.
- **Student Privacy Compass** — Senior Fellow (FPF's education privacy resource hub).
