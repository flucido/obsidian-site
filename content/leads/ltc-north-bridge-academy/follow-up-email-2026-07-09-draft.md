**One thing worth flagging now**

It's a small cleanup task; I just want it on our radar now rather than discovering it the morning of rollout.

**Where I'd suggest we focus Phase 1**

Based on what you both shared, here's the path I'd recommend we lock in for Phase 1, in this order:

1. **Reorganize Mosyle by Lower School and Middle School.** That gives every teacher a sensible default for which apps land on which iPads, and lets us personalize later for individual students who need specific configurations. You mentioned this is the partition you had in mind — I think it's the right one.
2. **Turn on Apple Classroom for middle school.** That's the live-screen-view piece middle school teachers have been asking for. It pairs with Mosyle for app deployment and gives teachers a single workflow.
3. **Set up a filtering policy that lets teachers control YouTube access per class.** Selective YouTube is one of those things that's genuinely useful in some lessons and genuinely distracting in others. We'd build the policy in Mosyle and let teachers toggle it themselves.
4. **Clean up Apple School Manager so all your faculty Macs are enrolled.** Small task, big payoff — once it's clean, the August iPad rollout can run on autopilot.

I'd plan to do (1) and (4) in the next couple of weeks, and (2) and (3) once Erin is back on campus week of 7/27 so we can test the classroom piece in a real classroom with real students before the August push.

- The Logitech keyboards you mentioned — I want to capture which models are currently in use so I can do a quick compatibility check. No purchase commitment; just research on my side.
- The Procreate app deployment — whether you'd want it per-student licenses through Apple School Manager, or shared-cart. We can decide that one closer to August.

I'll plan to send the initial inventory list as a separate attachment once I've refined it from what you pulled day-prior, Erin. That gives us a working document for the device-group cleanup rather than a one-shot snapshot.
