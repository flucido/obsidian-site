**Frank's call:** raise this in **the next full-engagement planning conversation** — not the 7/7 11 AM walkthrough, not in the Phase 1 SOW. Capture as a follow-up topic.

Google Workspace admin is a parallel track on a different vendor's console — different login, different admin model, different decision-makers (Erin / Annie / Dave may not all have Google admin access).
