## Discovery Questions (Annotated)

### Opening (2 min)
> "Lauren thought it'd be worth connecting — what made her think of me for North Bridge?"

This surfaces the unstated need. Let Annie/Shannon define the problem.

### Current State (5 min)
> "Can you walk me through what technology looks like at North Bridge right now? Student information, parent communication, admissions, fundraising — what tools are you using?"

Map the tech landscape before suggesting anything.

### Pain Points (5 min)
> "What's the thing that makes you say 'there has to be a better way'?"

Get to the real friction. Listen for emotional language — that's where the budget lives.

### Vision (3 min)
> "If we fixed the right thing this summer, what would be different when school starts in the fall?"

Timeline framing. Summer is the window for school projects.

### Decision Process (2 min)
> "If this conversation leads somewhere, who else needs to be in the room? Board? Business Director?"

Identify the real buyer. Katherine Monge Arriaza (Business/HR Director) and Julia Myers (Asst. Head) may be relevant.
