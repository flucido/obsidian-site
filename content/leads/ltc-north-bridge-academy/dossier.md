## Mosyle Research (6/16)

Confirmed pricing and capabilities via mosyle.com, school.mosyle.com:

| Tier | Price | Key Features |
|------|-------|--------------|
| **Free** | $0/year, no device limit | Full Apple MDM for iOS/iPadOS, shared iPads, SSO, parental controls |
| **Premium** | $5.50/device/year | + Classroom management, automated workflows, screen sharing, all Apple platforms |
| **OneK12** | $9.00/device/year | + Encrypted DNS web filtering, endpoint security, Mosyle Auth 2 (macOS SSO) |

**1:1 Deployment:** Zero-touch via Apple Business Manager — devices auto-enroll, no manual config.
**Filtering:** Only in OneK12 tier. Encrypted DNS-based, content categories + threat protection + CIPA compliance. GoGuardian may be redundant at this tier.
**Classroom management:** Teachers set class preferences once, deploy to all devices with one click. No IT involvement day-to-day.
**Scale:** 25,000+ schools, education-native since 2016.
**North Bridge estimate:** ~$0–270/year for ~30 iPads. Free tier may cover basic MDM needs.
