## Lead → Contact Promotion (back-burner)

**Frank's note from after-meeting:** Now that Frank is doing business with both North Bridge (signed/active engagement) and Susan Allen (SOW sent, awaiting signature, signature imminent), Frank wants a clear **lead → contact** promotion step with a **qualification gate.** Sketch only — don't act on it during the walkthrough.

### Why this matters

- **Leads** = prospects. Energy goes to discovery, qualification, outreach, scoring.
- **Contacts** = active engagements / clients / paying relationships. Energy goes to delivery, retention, expansion.
- Without a promotion step, both North Bridge and Susan keep showing up under "Active Opportunities" as if they're still being qualified, even after a contract is signed.
- The pipeline dashboards already have post-qualification stages (`Active Build`, `Support`, `Closed`) — but those are engagement-stage labels, not a "this is now a contact" marker. The promotion is a different cut.

### Likely qualification gate (sketch — refine later)

When does a Lead become a Contact? Sketch criteria — refine when Frank has bandwidth:

1. **Contract / SOW signed** (North Bridge: SOW draft accepted; Susan: SOW sent 7/6, awaiting signature).
2. **First deliverable scheduled or in-flight** (kick-off call, on-site visit, content handoff).
3. **Decision-maker + coordinator both named and reachable.**
4. **Engagement model settled** (project-based vs ongoing; flat fee vs hourly).
5. **Financial terms confirmed** (price, payment terms, billing contact).

Two-of-five is probably enough; three-of-five is safer.
